About the Company

Horizon Integration is a Saudi infrastructure and execution company specialized in roads, asphalt, drainage systems, external works, road marking, earthworks, civil execution and urban development support works.

The company operates through a practical delivery model built around equipment readiness, execution discipline, technical coordination and reliable site performance.
